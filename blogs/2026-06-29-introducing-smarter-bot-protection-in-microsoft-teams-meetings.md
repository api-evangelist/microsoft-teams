Announces a new admin policy for managing external bots in Teams meetings that detects bots, places them in the lobby, and requires explicit organizer confirmation before admission. Covers behavioral/infrastructure-based bot detection, lobby grouping for visibility, and confirmation safeguards, with future plans for allow lists and org-wide blocking policies.
