By: Ihor Lukyanenko, Sachin Jain, Kappa Ramlal, Abdul Rahim Charif, and Shangwei Fang Every day, hundreds of millions of people depend on Microsoft Teams to connect with colleagues, share ideas, make decisions, and move work forward. Whether they are jumping into a quick chat between meetings, joining a customer call that cannot be missed, or using Copilot to accomplish more, people rely on Teams to be ready the moment they need it. That trust is what drives our continued investment in performance.
