Today, we’re excited to announce the tech preview of the new Microsoft Teams VDI optimization for Omnissa Horizon environments , bringing a modern, high-performance collaboration experience to customers running Teams in virtual desktops. This milestone, achieved in close collaboration with our counterparts from Omnissa’s engineering team, marks the next step in Microsoft’s broader journey to deliver feature parity, reliability, and performance consistency between physical and virtual desktops , building on the architectural direction we first outlined in The future of Microsoft Teams in VDI .
