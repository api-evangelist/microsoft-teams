Microsoft Teams Rooms gives organizations the flexibility to create high-quality collaboration experiences across meeting spaces of every kind and size. Often we hear from customers, “which device or which platform should I choose?” The way we think about it is simple: we believe that customers should be empowered to expect high-quality collaboration experiences in any physical space, regardless of what platform they prefer, or the devices they install. We designed the Teams Rooms ecosystem with flexibility and optionality in mind, and those principles still guide us today.
